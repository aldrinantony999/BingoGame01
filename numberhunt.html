🌐 Online Number Hunt

🔒 Privacy: online play connects players directly, so the other player’s device can see your IP address. Your name is shared only with the person in your room. This game stores nothing on a server; a free public service (PeerJS) only helps the devices find each other.

One player creates a room and shares the 4-letter code; the other joins. Both find the same numbers on the same grid at the same time. The time you take becomes your opponent’s points, so the faster player earns more.
