👥 Play with friends

Fill all 25 boxes on your own card first (tap boxes in order, or Fill randomly). The host taps Start game. Players then take turns choosing a number: it is crossed on every card in the room and spoken aloud. Only the player whose turn it is can choose. The first to complete 5 lines wins.

🌐 Online XOX

One player taps Create room and shares the 4-letter code. The other types it and taps Join. The host plays X. Each player has 3 marks: drag them onto the board, then drag them to move. Get three in a row to win.
