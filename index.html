BINGO

Tap an empty box to place the next number (1, 2, 3…). Tap a filled box to clear it; that number becomes the next one to place. When all 25 boxes are filled, create or join a room to play.

Everyone fills all 25 boxes on their own card first. The host taps Start game. Players then take turns choosing a number: it is crossed on every card in the room. Only the player whose turn it is can choose. The first to complete 5 lines wins.
